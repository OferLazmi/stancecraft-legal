Stancecraft

Dress them. Pose them. Draw them.

A cast of 3D people to dress, pose and light, for figure drawing, character design, comics and manga. For Android.

A cast of figures

Choose from realistic, wooden mannequin, low-poly, casual clothed, anime and chibi figures. Nine are in the free version, with a pose library to get started fast.

  • Up to three figures in one scene
  • Mirror a pose left to right

Hands and faces

Curl each hand and pick from hand poses when you need a clear reference. Set facial expressions from a smile to surprise, then tune the intensity with a slider.

Light and camera

Place a key light and a fill light, with a ground shadow under the figure. Orbit the camera and change the field of view when you want foreshortening.

Scenes and props

Build a scene with 32 props — chairs, stairs, a sofa, a ladder, a sword, an umbrella and more. Put your own photo or a built-in backdrop behind the figure.

  • Save scenes to reopen later
  • Export a PNG with or without the background

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, the app and nine figures are free to use. A one-time Pro purchase (not a subscription) adds more figures, outfits and poses, a rim light, mesh export and Photo to Pose (beta).

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up.

Does it work offline?

Posing works offline.

Which languages?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Japanese, Korean and Hebrew.

What data does the app collect?

Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports through Google Firebase, to fix bugs and improve the app. Photos you use stay on your device. See the privacy policy and how to delete your data.
