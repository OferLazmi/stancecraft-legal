Privacy Policy - Stancecraft

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Stancecraft ("the app") is an offline 3D pose-reference app for artists. It has no account and no server, and it does not send your data anywhere. The app does not have the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot open a network connection.

What stays on your device

  • Scenes and poses: scenes, poses, body settings and prop layouts are saved as files in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Background photos: a photo you pick for the background or for Photo to Pose is copied into the app's private storage. The app does not ask for storage permission, and the photo is never uploaded.
  • Exports: images you export are saved to Pictures/Stancecraft or passed to the share sheet you choose. Those files are yours.
  • Photo to Pose: pose detection runs entirely on your device using Google's MediaPipe library. Photos are not sent anywhere.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro purchase is handled by Google Play. The app asks the Play Store app on your device whether Pro is owned and remembers the answer so Pro keeps working offline. Payment details are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; Stancecraft never sees them. The Play Billing library adds the BILLING and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions so it can talk to the Play Store app.

What we do not collect

Stancecraft does not collect your name, email, photos, poses, device identifiers, location, or usage statistics. There are no analytics, no ads, and no tracking. Nothing is shared with third parties.

Deleting your data

All app data lives only on your device. You can delete scenes inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Because there is no account or server, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Stancecraft is a drawing reference tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
