Privacy Policy - Stancecraft

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Stancecraft ("the app") is a 3D pose-reference app for artists. It has no account and no server of its own. Your scenes, poses and photos stay on your device. From version 0.6.2 the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes, so it has the Android INTERNET permission. What that sends is described below.

What stays on your device

  • Scenes and poses: scenes, poses, body settings and prop layouts are saved as files in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Background photos: a photo you pick for the background or for Photo to Pose is copied into the app's private storage. The app does not ask for storage permission, and the photo is never uploaded.
  • Exports: images you export are saved to Pictures/Stancecraft or passed to the share sheet you choose. Those files are yours.
  • Photo to Pose: pose detection runs entirely on your device using Google's MediaPipe library. Photos are not sent anywhere.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The app sends the following to Google Firebase (Google LLC), over an encrypted HTTPS connection:

  • App activity: which screens and panels you open and in-app events such as adding a figure, exporting an image, starting or finishing a Pro purchase or restore (result only), using a backdrop, and whether Photo to Pose found a pose. Event names and counts only.
  • Crash and performance data: crash and error reports from Firebase Crashlytics (stack traces, app version, device model, Android version).
  • Identifiers: a random app-instance ID created by Firebase. The app does not collect the Android advertising ID and does not use data for ads.

The app never sends your photos, poses, scenes, exported images, name, email or location. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase's privacy and security terms, and keeps it according to the project's Firebase retention settings.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro purchase is handled by Google Play. The app asks the Play Store app on your device whether Pro is owned and remembers the answer so Pro keeps working offline. Payment details are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; Stancecraft never sees them. The Play Billing library adds the BILLING and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions so it can talk to the Play Store app.

What we do not collect

Stancecraft does not collect your name, email, photos, poses, scenes, contacts, location, or the advertising ID. There are no ads and no cross-app tracking, and nothing is sold. The only third party that receives data is Google, for the Firebase analytics and crash reports described above.

Deleting your data

Your scenes and photos live only on your device. You can delete scenes inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Uninstalling also stops any new analytics or crash reports. To ask for deletion of analytics and crash data already sent, email oferlzm@gmail.com.

Children

Stancecraft is a drawing reference tool and is not directed at children.
